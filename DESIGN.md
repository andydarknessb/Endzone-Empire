# Design System: Endzone Empire

The values here are a readable mirror of `src/theme/tokens.js`, which stays the single source of truth. When the two disagree, tokens.js wins and this file is stale. The frontmatter lists the dark theme under the plain name and the light theme under a `-light` suffix or its own name.

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Commissioner's Desk"**

Endzone Empire is where a league gets run, week after week. The everyday surfaces are a working desk: dense, orderly, and quiet enough to make a lineup or waiver decision in seconds. Hairline cards, tabular numbers, and condensed display type keep a lot of data readable at once. Color on the desk is mostly a data encoding (positions, grades, home and away), not decoration.

The noise lives in the moments. Live games, a final score, a draft grade, a win, a trade landing: these are where the system gets loud and playful, with Endzone Green, Fourth-Quarter Amber on a scoreboard that stays black in both themes, big condensed figures, and the Tecmo-style sprite. A screen earns its loudness from what is happening in the league, never from ornament.

Home at `/user` and every league page (`/league/:leagueId/*`) are moving to the Tecmo play-select world in its own section below (owner decisions, 2026-10-15; ADR 0067). Home has shipped; the league pages move in five waves, and waves 1 to 3 have landed (the dashboard, Matchup, Game Center, Pick'em, Lineup, Waivers and Trade Center). Until wave 5 lands three generations coexist. The island's `dash-*` tokens, Barlow Condensed and Archivo remain only for the `shared/ui` pieces that surfaces not yet moved still compose (the Draft room among them); no new surface is drawn there, and no moved page uses them. **Legacy** pages under `src/components/` use the app tokens and MUI's Inter ramp. Each generation keeps its own tokens; never mix them on one surface.

The system rejects the sportsbook look (odds boards, neon promos, betting urgency) and the cluttered legacy-portal look (ad-shaped boxes, tiny type, every widget competing).

**Key Characteristics:**
- Dark-first and light-complete: every screen ships in both themes from the same token names.
- Condensed display type for names, scores, and stat values; tabular numerals on every figure.
- Flat island cards with a hairline edge; depth comes from tonal surface steps.
- Color encodes data first; the accent is reserved for primary action and live state.
- Contrast is asserted in tests, not eyeballed.

## Colors

A night-field palette of blue-black surfaces with one vivid green, plus a fixed amber-on-black scoreboard.

### Primary
- **Endzone Green**: the island's accent. Primary buttons, the live chip, the "You" pill, and active state. It flips to a deep green in light mode, because the bright dark-mode value cannot carry text on a near-white card. Its soft tint and line (12% and about 35% alpha) mark live and selected states.

### Secondary
- **Signal Blue**: the app accent on legacy pages, links, the focus ring, and the home side of a matchup. It is lightened in dark mode so its hover tint clears AA on the raised app bar.
- **Sideline Green**: the app secondary and success color, and the away side of a matchup. The brand wordmark gradient runs from Signal Blue to Sideline Green.

### Tertiary
- **Fourth-Quarter Amber**: LED digits on the Game Center scoreboard. It sits only on Scoreboard Black, which stays dark in both themes, because a scoreboard is black.
- **Caution Gold**: warnings, the Bench what-if gain chip, and the gold medal.
- **Flag Red**: danger, errors, and the Live pill on the scoring strip.

### Neutral
- **Night Field**: the island page background in dark mode, and the dark ink painted on grade chips and the primary button.
- **Desk Surface, Desk Tile, Desk Raised**: the three dark surface steps (card, stat tile, raised tile). Depth on the island is these steps, not shadows.
- **Chalk Ink, Dim Ink, Faint Ink**: dark-mode text tiers. Faint Ink is the lightest muted tier that still clears AA as body text.
- **Field Edge**: the edge of inputs, selects, steppers, and unselected choice cards, at the 3:1 that WCAG 1.4.11 asks of a boundary. Decorative hairlines cannot stand in for it.
- **Morning Field, Paper Surface, Paper Tile, Slate Ink, Slate Dim**: the light-mode counterparts.

### Data encodings
Position colors (`pos-qb` red, `pos-rb` green, `pos-wr` blue, `pos-te` orange, `pos-k` purple, `pos-def` gray, `pos-idp` teal) and grade colors (A green through F red) are data, not decoration. Each carries an AA label in both themes, and grade text has its own `-text` tokens, separate from the chip fills.

### Named Rules
**The One Truth Rule.** No component hard-codes a color literal. A missing color becomes a new semantic token in `tokens.js`, with its contrast pairing registered in `tokens.contrast.test.js`.

**The Island Boundary Rule.** A surface uses one generation's tokens only: Play-select surfaces use only `ps-*` tokens, island surfaces only `dash-*`, and legacy pages and the app shell (Nav, Footer) only app tokens.

**The Tint Placement Rule.** A tinted pill or chip sits only on the surfaces its pairing is registered for. The light danger pill never sits on Desk Raised's light counterpart, and the warning chip sits only on a card or a stat tile.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Barlow Condensed (with Impact, sans-serif)
**Body Font:** Archivo (with Helvetica Neue, Arial, sans-serif)
**Legacy Font:** Inter (with Roboto, Helvetica, Arial), on legacy pages only

**Character:** A condensed sports face set against a sturdy grotesque. Barlow carries the noise (names, scores, stats); Archivo keeps the desk readable. Both are self-hosted woff2 files and never fetched from Google Fonts at runtime.

### Hierarchy
- **Display** (Barlow Condensed 700, 30px, tabular numerals): projected and live scores on a matchup side.
- **Headline** (Barlow Condensed 700, 24px, line height 1.1, 0.02em tracking): team names and stat-tile values.
- **Body** (Archivo 400, 15px, line height 1.5): running text on island surfaces.
- **Button** (Archivo 600, 13px, line height 1.2): island button labels, never uppercased.
- **Label** (Archivo 600, 11px, 0.07em tracking, uppercase): stat-tile keys and small section labels, in Faint Ink.
- **Label large** (Archivo 600, 12px): chip text, card counts and tails, captions, and the deadline sub-line in the to-do list.
- **Body small** (Archivo 400, 14px): dim helper text, link and button labels inside cards, and filter chips. Grade chips are the one 14px use set in Barlow Condensed.
- **Body large** (Archivo 400, 16px): a to-do row's title (weight 600) and the greeting summary line on desktop.
- **Title** (Barlow Condensed 600, 18px, 0.08em tracking, uppercase): feed and highlight card titles, and the to-do deadline value on phones.
- **Headline small** (Barlow Condensed 700, 22px, tabular numerals): the league-code input. The invite code on the created-league screen is also 22px but uses Code.
- **Headline medium** (Barlow Condensed 700, 26px): section and dialog titles, the empty-state and caught-up titles on phones, and the stat-tile value.
- **Panel title** (Barlow Condensed 600, 20px, line height 1.2, 0.08em tracking, uppercase): the h2 of a Home panel such as Needs your attention or Next draft.
- **Greeting** (Barlow Condensed 700, 48px from md up and 36px on phones, line height 1, 0.01em tracking, uppercase): the Home page's one h1, "Welcome back, name".
- **Display small** (Barlow Condensed 700, 28px): the league name on the Next draft card and the create-league stepper title on phones.
- **Code** (ui-monospace, SFMono-Regular, Consolas, monospace, 600, 0.1em tracking): the invite-code input and the league-code display and input; the size follows the field.
- **Legacy ramp** (Inter): h1 2.5rem/800 down to h6 1rem/600; buttons 600 with no text transform; the `stat` variant (tabular numerals, 600) for any score or points figure.

### Named Rules
**The Tabular Figures Rule.** Every score, point total, projection, and stat is set in tabular numerals so columns line up.

**The Copy House Style Rule.** No em dashes in user-visible copy; use middot separators, hyphen scores, and en-dash ranges (ADR 0016, enforced by `npm run guards`).

## Layout

The spacing scale runs in 4px steps up to 16px, then 24, 32, 48, and 64px (`space-1` to `space-8`). Island card grids auto-fit columns at a 180px minimum with a 14px gap. Card headers pad 14px by 18px over a hairline divider and wrap their title, count, and tail at phone width instead of squeezing them.

Breakpoints are MUI's defaults (sm 600px, md 900px, lg 1200px, xl 1536px). Below md, interactive targets grow to 44px; island buttons are 38px from md up.

Density is high by design: a commissioner's desk shows a lot at once. Compact rows, striped tables, and opaque sticky columns keep scanning fast without crowding.

## Elevation & Depth

The island is flat. Depth comes from stepping between surface tones (page, card, stat tile, raised tile) with hairline edges, not from shadows. Legacy MUI cards carry a light shadow at rest, and Paper elevation maps onto three shadow tokens that differ by theme.

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **shadow-1** (light `0 1px 2px rgba(16, 24, 32, 0.08)`, dark `0 1px 2px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.45)`): legacy cards at rest.
- **shadow-2** (light `0 4px 12px rgba(16, 24, 32, 0.10)`, dark `0 6px 16px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.5)`): menus and raised Paper.
- **shadow-3** (light `0 12px 28px rgba(16, 24, 32, 0.16)`, dark `0 14px 32px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.6)`): dialogs and the highest Paper.

### Named Rules
**The Flat Island Rule.** Island cards never take a shadow. To lift something, step it to the next surface tone.

## Shapes

Corners are gently rounded and consistent per generation: island cards at 14px, island buttons at 9px, legacy cards at 16px, legacy buttons and inputs at 10px, and small elements at 6px. Chips, badges, pills, and scrollbar thumbs are full pills (999px). Edges are 1px hairlines (`dash-line`, about 12% alpha) on cards; inputs take the stronger Field Edge.

## Components

### Buttons
Compact and confident: the island's DashButton is the one canonical implementation.
- **Shape:** gently rounded (9px), 38px tall from md up and 44px below it; the `sm` size is 32px.
- **Primary:** Night Field label on an Endzone Green fill with a matching border. Hover keeps the fill and brightens it slightly (`filter: brightness(1.08)`, eased over 150ms).
- **Ghost:** Dim Ink label, transparent fill, a strong hairline border. Hover moves the label to Chalk Ink and the border to the green accent line.
- **Disabled:** the primary drops its fill for Dim Ink on Desk Raised, so it never reads as live.
- **Legacy:** MUI buttons at 10px radius, weight 600, no elevation, transitions on background, border, and shadow over 150ms.

### Chips
- **Style:** full pills at weight 600. Live chips use accent text on the accent tint with the accent line; Final uses success text on the away tint.
- **Data chips:** position and grade chips are filled with their encoding color and carry the fixed AA label.

### Cards / Containers
- **Corner Style:** 14px on the island, 16px legacy.
- **Background:** Desk Surface on the island (Paper Surface in light); stat tiles step to Desk Tile.
- **Shadow Strategy:** none on the island; shadow-1 on legacy cards (see Elevation & Depth).
- **Border:** a 1px hairline.
- **Internal Padding:** a header row at 14px by 18px over a divider, then body content on the spacing scale.

### Inputs / Fields
- **Style:** a Field Edge boundary that meets 3:1 against the page, a card, or a stat tile.
- **Focus:** the global ring, a 2px solid outline in the focus-ring color offset by 2px, on every focusable control in both themes.

### Navigation
The app shell (Nav, Footer) stays in the app generation with app tokens, even when it frames an island surface.

### Scoreboard
The Game Center scoreboard view: Fourth-Quarter Amber LED digits in Barlow Condensed on Scoreboard Black, with a dimmed amber for unlit segments. It stays dark in both themes.

### Stat Tile
An uppercase Label key in Faint Ink above a Headline-size value in tabular Barlow Condensed, with an optional small Archivo unit in Dim Ink.

## Play-select: Home and league pages

Home (`/user`) is a play-call screen: a near-black field, NES-blue play cards with white X-and-O diagrams, double-ruled frames, and Press Start 2P for display. Scope is Home and every league page (ADR 0067); a page uses these tokens only once its wave has shipped, and never mixes them with `dash-*` or app tokens. The app shell keeps the app tokens, and Game Center's amber retro scoreboard stays amber. The `ps-*` frontmatter colors list dark under the plain name and light under a `-light` suffix. Colors with no `-light` entry (play, play-field, play-ink, play-dim, and the four board colors) are the same in both themes.

**Spec status.** The design canvas (10 boards, published version 27 at https://claude.ai/artifact/MU9fvovmpKB5cSD5eExJWe) is the spec; its working files are in `docs/design/home/`. Shipped in #2315: the colors are the `ps-*` tokens (named `home-*` until ADR 0067 wave 1a) in `src/theme/tokens.js` (plus `ps-selection` and `ps-flash` for text selection and the score flash), with their contrast pairs in `tokens.contrast.test.js`, and the generic primitives live in `src/shared/ui/playselect/` while the Home-only pieces stay in `src/components/UserPage/home/`. The Create league and Join league dialogs (#2454) are on the kit: the 6px double frame on the Field scrim, a pixel title over a 2px rule, the one forward action a fill, Back and Cancel ghosts, and refusals in the Danger edge inside the dialog.

### Palette (both themes)
- **Field** (ps-field): the page ground, near-black in dark and pale lavender-white in light. **Panel** (ps-panel): the fill inside frames.
- **Frame** (ps-frame): the double rule, pale periwinkle in dark and the play blue in light. **Rule** (ps-rule): the 2px inner dividers. **Frame 2** (ps-frame-2): the 2px edge of a tertiary frame, the Rule colour on the dark Field and the Frame colour on the lavender one.
- **Ink** and **Dim** (ps-ink, ps-dim): text tiers on the page and panels.
- **Play, Play field, Play ink, Play dim**: the card's NES-blue fill, its darker field band, the white diagram and name, and the pale blue for league lines and hash marks. **Play accent** with **On play accent** is the field green of the filled foot and the Live lock line on the card. Fixed in both themes, like the scoreboard.
- **Accent** (ps-accent) with **On accent**: field green in dark and the deep green in light; the one filled action and live state.
- **Cursor** (ps-cursor): yellow in dark and a dark gold in light; the selected play's frame only.
- **Danger** with **On danger**: red for the urgent strip, the urgent cursor, and error panels.
- **Board** family: black, fixed in both themes, with white ink, a dim ink, and a rule, for the live scoreboard and pick'em tiles. **Skel** is the loading block.

### Type
Press Start 2P (display) is set at 16, 24, and 32px only, on an 8px grid, uppercase, line height 1.5 (1.3 on the 32px h1). 32px is the desktop h1 and big scores, 24px is the phone h1, phone scores, and the desktop h2, and 16px is the phone h2, play-card names, tile titles, lock times, and chip and badge text. Anything the pixel face is asked to set smaller snaps up to 16px; there are no other sizes. Archivo is the body at 16px, with 15px (600) for button and link labels, 14px for league lines, lock notes, and captions, and 13px for design notes and small meta. Pixel type is for headings, scores, card names, and lock times; anything read as a sentence stays Archivo.

### Frame
The one frame is a 6px double rule in Frame color with square corners and Panel fill, with a 2px solid Rule divider under its header (header padding 14px by 20px, 12px by 14px on phones). The scoreboard tile and the error panel use the same 6px double rule, in Board dim and Danger. There are no radii and no shadows on Home.

### Play card
The signature: a column of an optional urgent strip, the field band, the body, and the foot.
- **Field band:** Play field fill between two 2px dashed Play dim rules, holding a pixel pictogram (96 by 48 viewBox on a 2px grid, white, crisp edges) drawn from X and O sprites, stepped routes, and arrowheads. One pictogram per play kind: picks, lineup, draft, waivers, trade, trophy, create, join. 144px wide on desktop, 96px on phones.
- **Body:** the play name in Pixel 16, the league and detail line in Play dim, then the lock day and time in Pixel 16 with a one-line lock note.
- **Foot:** a 44px bar pinned to the card bottom with a 2px Play ink border. The lead card's foot is the one filled action (Play accent fill, On play accent label, weight 700). Every other foot is outlined in Play ink. A play across several leagues is a group with one foot per league, and only the first can be filled.
- **Urgent strip:** under one hour, a Danger band across the card top with a pixel clock icon and "38 min left" in On danger.
- **Cursor:** the lead card (see The One Cursor Rule) sits in a 4px Cursor-colored frame with 8px of padding around the card. Under one hour the frame turns Danger. Non-lead cards keep the same padding with a transparent border so nothing shifts.

### Odds bar
Win probability as 20 blocks, 12px tall and 2px apart, each block worth 5 points. Filled blocks are Accent only while the matchup is live and Frame color before kickoff. Empty blocks are a 2px Dim outline. On the scoreboard tile the bar sits on Board with a bright green fill. The bar is `role="img"` with the percent in its label, with a pixel percent and "win probability · Endzone Forecast" under it.

### Scoreboard tile and pick'em tile
A live matchup or pick'em league on Board black with the 6px double rule in Board dim. The title is Pixel 16, then a status line in Board dim, then the score block as one link: You and opponent figures in Pixel 32 (24 on phones) with the leader in white and the trailer in Board dim, the margin in Pixel 16, a "Matchup" cue with a pixel arrow, and the odds bar. The league's other links run underneath in the fixed order Matchup, Lineup, Picks, Waivers, keeping only those the league has, divided by a Board rule. The pick'em tile swaps the score block for one Pixel 32 figure (points, or a record in straight-up leagues) with its label and a "Picks" cue.

### Buttons, links, chips
Buttons are square, 44px tall, 2px bordered, Archivo 600 at 15px with a pixel arrow: Fill (Accent), Ghost (Ink border and label), and Quiet (transparent border, Dim label). Links are 44px targets in Ink with a 2px Dim underline offset 5px. The Live chip is a 2px Accent outline with Accent Pixel 16 text and a square 8px dot. Count badges are 28px squares with a 2px Ink border.

### Focus and motion
- **Focus ring:** a 3px solid white outline with no offset and a 7px halo in the dark Field color (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 7px`), on every focusable control in both themes. Text selection is cursor yellow on dark Field.
- **Cursor blink:** the cursor frame's border blinks off and on three times (0.5s, stepped), then rests lit. Under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` the blink is off. Hover brightens a button or link by 12% (`filter: brightness(1.12)`).
- **Cursor move:** hover (on pointer devices) or keyboard focus inside a play card moves the cursor frame to that card at once, in the lead's colour if it is the lead and in Cursor yellow otherwise, and clears the frame from the lead. When the pointer or focus leaves, the cursor goes back to the lead.
- **Press:** while a play card is pressed its frame turns white and its content drops 2px. Under reduced motion the frame still turns white but the content does not move.
- **Score flash:** when a refresh changes a live score, the score block flashes a 16% white wash three times (0.3s, stepped), then holds. Under reduced motion it holds the wash steadily for 1s instead. Nothing else on Home animates.

### League pages
The canvas at https://claude.ai/artifact/PrJq3Dhr94dkYbkG4hw2Vj (version 12, working files and the full rulings log in `docs/design/league/`) is the spec for every league page; ADR 0067 sets the waves. The pieces below extend Home's.
- **Page skeleton:** pixel h1 (the league name), a chip row (the phase chip, a team-count chip only outside the live season, Draft Complete on complete fantasy leagues, the Live chip on game day) and an Archivo "Times in CDT" line; then Your move, then the page's main board, then secondary frames. No new navigation: each page restyles the navigation it has (breadcrumbs, back links).
- **Your move:** the lead play card on the viewer's next move in this league, with non-lead plays as compact cards beside it (name, lock, foot; no field band). Its note names the reason and the fix ("Start Jordan Addison for Puka Nacua · +23.0 pts"). The badge counts the cards. When nothing is due it is a calm "You're set" card with no cursor and no filled action.
- **Container tiers:** Board black for live state; the 6px double rule for primary frames (Your move, Matchup, My team, Standings); a 2px rule with a Pixel 16 h2 for tertiary frames (Recap, Around the league, Recent activity, Pick'em, Commissioner). Primary h2s are Pixel 24 on desktop and 16 on phones.
- **Live scoreboard:** Board black edge to edge, both sides on two equal columns, names on one baseline, the margin ("UP 2.1") under the viewer's score, "As of 2:40 PM" under the Live chip, a full-width live odds bar, and a polite live region carrying the score sentence. On game day it sits directly under Your move, and it leads the page when no lock is under an hour.
- **Standings screen:** rank, team, W-L-T, PF, PA, STRK in tabular figures on 44px rows; the viewer's row is Play-blue with a pixel arrow (not a cursor); a dashed Frame rule marks the playoff line. Phones show the viewer's row with two teams either side and a "Full standings" link.
- **Tags:** position tags are a 2px border and text in the position colour (data encodings, exempt from the red and green rules); injury tags are outlined ("Q", "D") or a filled Ink "OUT"; activity kind tags use the product's kinds. Chips with a status word are Pixel 16; in-row badges are Archivo 13 700 uppercase. Nothing is set below 13px.
- **Starter state marks (Matchup Starters, #2395):** In progress is a solid 12px square in Danger that blinks lit then dim on a 1s stepped cycle (it holds lit under reduced motion; the legend sample never blinks); Final is a 12px 3x3 pixel checker in Ink; Yet to play is an empty 12px square with a 2px Dim outline. Desktop prints the word beside the mark; on phones the word is screen-reader-only and a starter cell is three lines (name and injury tag, mark and figure, game text), with "DET · Bye" on the game line for a starter with no opponent.
- **Tables and forms:** sticky first column and a scrolling box at phone width; buttons inside dense desktop tables may be 36px, every other control is 44px. Toggles, tabs and radio cards show their selected state with a word, dot or arrow as well as the Play-blue fill.
- **Say it once:** one label per destination ("Set lineup" as an action, "Lineup" in link rows, "Matchup" for the matchup page), one Endzone Forecast credit per frame, one lock time per fact.

### Named Rules
**The One Cursor Rule.** At most one play on a page wears the cursor frame, and a page where nothing needs the viewer has none: the earliest lock leads, and a live draft never outranks a pick that locks during it. Hover or focus moves that one cursor and never adds a second. A second cursor means neither is selected.

**The Steps Not Slides Rule.** Home motion jumps in stepped frames, as an 8-bit screen does. It never eases, fades or slides.

**The One Filled Action Rule.** One filled green button per page, on the lead play's foot. Every other control is ghost, quiet, or an underlined link.

**The Green Means Go Rule.** Accent green appears only on the one filled action and on live state (the live chip and the live odds bar). It never decorates, and an odds bar before kickoff is Frame color.

**The Red Is Rare Rule.** Danger red appears only when a lock is under one hour (the urgent strip and the cursor turned red), when a draft pick clock is under 30 seconds, for errors, and for the blinking light of a starter whose game is in progress (Matchup Starters, #2395, owner request 2026-10-20). A page with nothing urgent and no error has no red.

**The Shared Lock Rule.** Plays that share a lock are one card, not several (picks and a lineup locking at the same kickoff are one card with a foot each). One play across several leagues is a single card with one foot link per league.

**The Square Rule.** Play-select surfaces have no corner radius and no drop shadow. Depth is the double rule and the Play-blue fill against the Field.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** take every value from `src/theme/tokens.js`, and add a semantic token (and its contrast pairing) when one is missing.
- **Do** ship both themes for every screen, using each theme's own values and never a mix.
- **Do** set every figure in tabular numerals, with Barlow Condensed for scores and stat values on island surfaces.
- **Do** save loudness for live state and big moments: live pills, finals, grades, wins, the scoreboard.
- **Do** keep a visible 2px focus ring and honor the global reduced-motion policy, which makes transitions instant.
- **Do** use inline stroke SVG icons on a 20 or 24px grid.

### Don't:
- **Don't** hard-code a color literal in CSS, `sx`, or inline styles.
- **Don't** mix `dash-*` and app tokens on one surface.
- **Don't** put a shadow on an island card.
- **Don't** borrow sportsbook visuals: odds boards, neon promos, countdown urgency.
- **Don't** add legacy-portal clutter: ad-shaped boxes, tiny type, widgets competing for attention.
- **Don't** use emoji in the product, or em dashes in its copy.
- **Don't** use a position or grade color as decoration.
