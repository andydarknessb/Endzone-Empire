# A fantasy League opens in the first NFL week not yet kicked off when its draft completes

Status: accepted (2026-11-09)

Every fantasy League starts at 2026 week 1, the column default, whenever it is
created or drafted. A Player counts for a Team only in a game that kicked off
while the Team held him, so a League drafted after week 1 scores 0-0 for every
week already played, and a League created in 2027 plays a finished season.

We decide that a League's Opening week is the earliest NFL week with no kickoff
yet at the moment its draft completes, in the NFL season in progress or next to
start. Its regular season runs from that week to its last regular-season week,
so a late League plays a shorter schedule. A League with no regular-season week
left cannot start its draft, and the refusal tells the commissioner to schedule
it for next season.

## Considered options

- **Decide when the League is created.** Rejected: a League created in August
  and drafted in October would still open on weeks already played.
- **Refuse every draft once week 1 has kicked off.** Rejected: a group that
  forms in October could not play at all this year.
- **Play next season whenever the current one has begun.** Rejected: rosters
  would sit untouched for months, and the League would have nothing to do.

## Amendment (#2746, 2026-11-10)

A draft that started before the League's last regular-season week kicked off
and completes after it has no open week under the rule above. It opens the
League at that last regular-season week anyway and plays it from completion on.
Only Players picked before their own game's kickoff score, so that week can be
uneven between Teams. The start refusal bounds who can reach this: a League
whose every regular-season week has already kicked off cannot start its draft.
The rule above otherwise stands.
