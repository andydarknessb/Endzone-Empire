## Fantasy draft-stock risers

### De'Zhaun Stribling, WR, 49ers — late-round arrow up

The second-round rookie caught four of five targets for 46 yards in 14 snaps after producing seven catches for 63 yards in Week 1. The repeat production, limited-snap efficiency and San Francisco's receiver injuries support a meaningful stock increase, although the preseason sample does not establish a regular-season target share. ([NFL Thursday recap](https://www.nfl.com/news/2026-nfl-preseason-week-2-what-we-learned-thursday-games))

### Woody Marks, RB, Texans — backfield-depth arrow up

Marks rushed four times for 39 yards and a 20-yard touchdown; the NFL's drive accounting credited 43 yards on five touches and praised his pass protection while still identifying David Montgomery as Houston's lead back. That combination supports a rise behind Montgomery, not a claim that Marks has displaced him. ([NFL Texans recap](https://www.nfl.com/news/texans-depth-shines-narrow-preseason-loss-raiders))

### Mike Washington Jr., RB, Raiders — dynasty/deep-bench arrow up

The rookie ran nine times for 56 yards, beginning with a 33-yard carry. Ashton Jeanty remains ahead of him, so this is evidence for Washington's reserve-role case rather than ordinary redraft volume. ([Raiders game recap](https://www.raiders.com/news/game-recap-raiders-erase-17-point-deficit-outmuscle-texans-down-the-stretch))

### Jonathon Brooks, RB, Panthers — goal-line-role arrow up

Brooks carried five times for 18 yards and converted a 1-yard touchdown with Carolina's starters. The Panthers also documented an earlier missed pass-protection assignment, so the performance is a positive role signal rather than an unqualified breakout. ([Panthers rapid reactions](https://www.panthers.com/news/rapid-reactions-panthers-beat-the-jaguars-34-17-as-starters-shine); [Panthers Brooks feature](https://www.panthers.com/news/a-sign-to-my-father-jonathon-brooks-on-his-special-preseason-touchdown))

### Bo Nix, QB, Broncos — health and efficiency arrow up

In his first game since a broken ankle, Nix completed 6 of 9 passes for 76 yards and a touchdown across two scoring possessions. His explosive connections included a 35-yard catch-and-run by Jaylen Waddle and a 21-yard touchdown to RJ Harvey, and he reported no setback afterward. The healthy return is more consequential than the small preseason sample. ([NFL Nix report](https://www.nfl.com/news/broncos-bo-nix-preseason-return-ankle-injury-like-riding-a-bike))

### Shedeur Sanders, QB, Browns — competition arrow up

Sanders completed 9 of 11 passes for 74 yards with an 8-yard touchdown to Luke Floriea and one interception. Deshaun Watson completed 5 of 12 with an interception. Sanders' game was better, but the Browns had not named a starter and the coach said the decision would not rest on one game; treat this as movement within the competition, not proof Sanders won it. The yardage on Watson's line is intentionally omitted because the official NFL recap lists 34 and the Browns recap lists 36. ([NFL Saturday recap](https://www.nfl.com/news/2026-nfl-preseason-week-2-what-we-learned-saturday-games); [Browns game recap](https://www.clevelandbrowns.com/news/bills-31-browns-7-final-score-stats-game-recap))

### Colbie Young, WR, Bengals — late-round/deep-league arrow up

With Cincinnati's top three receivers held out, the fourth-round rookie ran 22 routes and drew eight targets from Josh Johnson, catching three passes for 48 yards and a 17-yard touchdown. All three receptions came on targets at least 10 yards downfield. The opportunity concentration and downfield role support a modest fantasy stock increase, while the three-catch result on eight targets argues against treating him as redraft-ready depth. ([NFL Saturday recap](https://www.nfl.com/news/2026-nfl-preseason-week-2-what-we-learned-saturday-games))

### Jacardia Wright, RB, Seahawks — roster-battle arrow up

Wright broke a 69-yard touchdown run and reached 20.93 mph, according to the NFL's Next Gen Stats data cited in the league recap. The NFL noted that first-round rookie Jadarian Price had not played in the preseason, Zach Charbonnet was returning from a torn ACL, and Emanuel Wilson was nursing a hamstring injury; Wright's result strengthens his roster case but does not establish a regular-season role. ([NFL Sunday recap](https://www.nfl.com/news/2026-nfl-preseason-week-2-what-we-learned-seahawks-titans))

### Joe Milton III, QB, Cowboys — superflex/depth arrow up

Milton started while competing with Sam Howell for the QB2 job, completed 9 of 13 passes for 179 yards and two touchdowns, and added a 1-yard rushing score. His explosive completions included a 53-yard touchdown to Jonathan Mingo and a 70-yard gain to rookie Camden Brown; Howell later went 7 of 10 for 65 yards with an end-zone interception. This is strong evidence for movement in Dallas's backup competition, but Milton remains behind Dak Prescott and is not a conventional one-quarterback redraft target. ([NFL Saturday recap](https://www.nfl.com/news/2026-nfl-preseason-week-2-what-we-learned-saturday-games))

### Devin Singletary, RB, Giants — depth arrow up

Singletary produced 40 yards and a touchdown on eight carries while Tyrone Tracy Jr. had 26 yards on six carries and lost a fumble. The league recap described Singletary as the better performer in the game, supporting a relative stock increase within the Giants' reserve-back picture rather than a claim that he passed starter Cam Skattebo. ([NFL Saturday recap](https://www.nfl.com/news/2026-nfl-preseason-week-2-what-we-learned-saturday-games))

## Fantasy draft-stock losers

### Tyrone Tracy Jr., RB, Giants — role-security arrow down

Tracy entered behind Singletary, lost a fumble near the goal line, finished with six carries for 26 yards, and received only four additional touches over his final 14 snaps. He also allowed one pressure in three pass-blocking attempts and worked on the kickoff team. Those facts support concern about role security; the NFL recap itself framed his roster spot as potentially vulnerable after the Giants added Najee Harris. ([NFL Saturday recap](https://www.nfl.com/news/2026-nfl-preseason-week-2-what-we-learned-saturday-games))

### Cam Ward, QB, Titans — redraft arrow down

Against Seattle backups, Ward completed 8 of 12 passes for 69 yards without a touchdown or turnover and was pressured four times and sacked once. Tennessee's Ward-led run game gained 10 yards across three drives; the offense did not score a touchdown, while Joey Slye supplied four field goals. The NFL calculated Ward's two-game preseason line at 13 of 24 for 126 yards with no touchdowns or interceptions and six drives producing 10 points. ([NFL Sunday recap](https://www.nfl.com/news/2026-nfl-preseason-week-2-what-we-learned-seahawks-titans))

### Zach Wilson and Spencer Rattler, QB, Saints — backup-competition arrow down

Wilson opened 4 of 4 for 39 yards, then went 2 of 8 for 16 yards in the second quarter and threw an interception after a miscommunication with Bub Means. Rattler threw a red-zone pick-six to Rams safety Alex Cook, and New Orleans was shut out. The performance damages both quarterbacks' competition cases but has limited ordinary redraft relevance. ([NFL Saturday recap](https://www.nfl.com/news/2026-nfl-preseason-week-2-what-we-learned-saturday-games))

### Fernando Mendoza, QB, Raiders — protection/context warning

The 2026 No. 1 overall pick was hit five times, pressured on 37.5% of his dropbacks, sacked once, and threw an 80-yard pick-six to Texans rookie Wade Woodaz. The NFL also noted that Mendoza played without the Raiders' starting offensive line and did not appear rattled, so the fair fantasy reading is a developmental warning rather than a categorical downgrade. ([NFL Thursday recap](https://www.nfl.com/news/2026-nfl-preseason-week-2-what-we-learned-thursday-games))

### Pittsburgh's young quarterbacks — competition arrow down

Will Howard completed 10 of 15 passes for 74 yards with two interceptions. Rookie Drew Allar followed his three-touchdown Week 1 debut by completing 11 of 23 for 110 yards and taking three sacks of at least nine yards. The shutout weakens both backup bids but does not create normal one-quarterback fantasy value for the winner. ([NFL Steelers quarterback report](https://www.nfl.com/news/will-howard-drew-allar-young-steelers-qbs-subpar-preseason-shutout-loss))

### Colts backup quarterbacks — competition arrow down

Riley Leonard went 7 of 14 for 62 yards, and Anthony Richardson went 3 of 9 for 24 yards. Indianapolis produced six points while the two combined for 86 passing yards without a touchdown. This concerns the backup job behind Daniel Jones rather than an immediate standard-league target. ([Colts game recap](https://www.colts.com/news/offense-struggles-as-colts-drop-second-preseason-game-vs-atlanta-falcons))

## Standout rookies

### Joey Aguilar, QB, and Trebor Peña, WR, Jaguars

Aguilar completed 9 of 10 passes for 130 yards and a touchdown; the score was a 29-yard connection with Peña. Both are developmental/roster standouts rather than established standard-redraft targets. ([Jaguars game report](https://www.jaguars.com/news/game-report-2026-preseason-week-2-panthers-34-jaguars-17))

### De'Zhaun Stribling, WR, 49ers

Stribling's four catches, five targets, 46 yards and 14 snaps extended his productive start to the preseason and made him one of the week's strongest rookie receiving signals. ([NFL Thursday recap](https://www.nfl.com/news/2026-nfl-preseason-week-2-what-we-learned-thursday-games))

### Athan Kaliakmanis, QB, and Jaden Bradley, WR, Commanders

Kaliakmanis completed 78% of his throws for 162 yards without taking a sack, including a 55-yard completion to Nick Nash and a 30-yard completion to rookie Bradley. Bradley made the 30-yard gain with one hand in tight coverage. These are roster/practice-squad signals, not seasonal fantasy projections. ([Commanders takeaways](https://www.commanders.com/news/washington-commanders-detroit-lions-preseason-takeaways))

### Jack Strand, QB, Falcons

The undrafted rookie completed 12 of 17 passes for 212 yards and a touchdown, then added 22 yards and a touchdown on nine carries. Atlanta used second- and third-team players, limiting the result's normal redraft significance. ([Falcons game center](https://www.atlantafalcons.com/game-day/2026/pre/falcons-at-colts/); [Falcons game breakdown](https://www.atlantafalcons.com/news/game-breakdown-what-happened-in-falcons-preseason-game-vs-indianapolis-colts))

### Behren Morton, QB, Patriots

The seventh-round rookie completed 13 of 17 passes for 129 yards, one touchdown and a 117.0 passer rating, including a 37-yard game-winning touchdown with 57 seconds remaining. He also punted three times for 48, 43 and 42 yards. The unusual punting work is roster-flexibility context, not fantasy scoring value. ([NFL Saturday recap](https://www.nfl.com/news/2026-nfl-preseason-week-2-what-we-learned-saturday-games))

### Colbie Young, WR, Bengals

Young's 22 routes, eight targets, 48 receiving yards and touchdown were one of the weekend's clearest rookie opportunity signals; the five incompletions included three tight-window throws with less than one yard of separation. ([NFL Saturday recap](https://www.nfl.com/news/2026-nfl-preseason-week-2-what-we-learned-saturday-games))

### Ty Simpson, QB, Rams

Simpson played the second half of the Rams' shutout and completed 8 of 12 passes for 73 yards. He directed two 11-play possessions, one ending on downs and the other on a Jarquez Hunter rushing touchdown. This was composed follow-up work, not a repeat of the two-touchdown debut that preceded it. ([NFL Saturday recap](https://www.nfl.com/news/2026-nfl-preseason-week-2-what-we-learned-saturday-games))

### Camden Brown, WR, Cowboys

The undrafted rookie produced a 70-yard reception that set up Joe Milton III's 1-yard touchdown run. One explosive catch sustains Brown's roster-case momentum but does not by itself prove a stable target role. ([NFL Saturday recap](https://www.nfl.com/news/2026-nfl-preseason-week-2-what-we-learned-saturday-games))

## Individual defensive performances

### Wade Woodaz, LB, Texans

The rookie returned a Fernando Mendoza interception 80 yards for a touchdown. The play came as Houston's reserve defense pressured Mendoza on 37.5% of his dropbacks and hit him five times. ([NFL Thursday recap](https://www.nfl.com/news/2026-nfl-preseason-week-2-what-we-learned-thursday-games))

### David Bailey, edge, Jets

Bailey generated two pressures and a drive-ending sack on his first three rushes against Broderick Jones; Jones ultimately allowed five pressures and two sacks. ([NFL Friday recap](https://www.nfl.com/news/2026-nfl-preseason-week-2-what-we-learned-friday-games))

### Jack Jones and Ephesians Prysock, CB, 49ers

Jones broke up two passes, while rookie Prysock produced a third-down breakup in the second half. ([49ers game recap](https://www.49ers.com/news/49ers-defeat-chargers-41-17-in-preseason-week-2-5-takeaways-from-sfvslac))

### Patrick Jones II and Chau Smith-Wade, Panthers

Jones sacked Nick Mullens for an 8-yard loss; Smith-Wade intercepted Carter Bradley and set up a Carolina touchdown. ([Panthers rapid reactions](https://www.panthers.com/news/rapid-reactions-panthers-beat-the-jaguars-34-17-as-starters-shine))

### Damar Hamlin and Te'Cory Couch, DB, Bills

Hamlin intercepted a tipped pass and led Buffalo with five tackles. Couch intercepted Watson after Jordan Dunbar tipped the throw; Buffalo finished with two takeaways, seven pass breakups, five tackles for loss and four forced three-and-outs. ([NFL Saturday recap](https://www.nfl.com/news/2026-nfl-preseason-week-2-what-we-learned-saturday-games); [Bills takeaways](https://www.buffalobills.com/news/top-3-things-we-learned-from-bills-vs-browns-preseason-week-2-2026))

### Amik Robertson, Joshua Josephs and Quan Martin, Commanders

Robertson strip-sacked Josh Dobbs on Detroit's second snap, Josephs recovered the ball in the end zone for a touchdown, and Martin intercepted Dobbs six plays later. ([Commanders takeaways](https://www.commanders.com/news/washington-commanders-detroit-lions-preseason-takeaways))

### Mike Green, edge, Ravens

Green generated six pressures and one sack across J.J. McCarthy's 11 dropbacks. Baltimore's rush also sacked third-string quarterback Carson Wentz twice after McCarthy left. ([NFL Saturday recap](https://www.nfl.com/news/2026-nfl-preseason-week-2-what-we-learned-saturday-games))

### Anthony Hill Jr., LB, Titans

The rookie led Tennessee with seven tackles and intercepted a pass in the second half. His individual performance stood apart from a Titans starting defense that conceded 16 first-quarter points to Seattle's second-string offense. ([NFL Sunday recap](https://www.nfl.com/news/2026-nfl-preseason-week-2-what-we-learned-seahawks-titans))

### R Mason Thomas, edge, Chiefs

The second-round rookie recorded four quarterback pressures in 14 snaps. First-round rookie cornerback Mansoor Delane also influenced two early possessions in an 11-snap debut, drawing a retaliatory penalty from Ted Hurst and disrupting a screen that Chris Roland-Wallace finished for a loss. ([NFL Saturday recap](https://www.nfl.com/news/2026-nfl-preseason-week-2-what-we-learned-saturday-games))

### Alex Cook, S, Rams

Cook returned Spencer Rattler's red-zone interception for a touchdown, helping Los Angeles preserve a 34-0 shutout. ([NFL Saturday recap](https://www.nfl.com/news/2026-nfl-preseason-week-2-what-we-learned-saturday-games))

## Injuries and material updates

### Jayden Higgins, WR, Texans

Higgins tore his ACL during the August 18 joint practice and was reported out for the 2026 season. This was not a game injury, but it is a decisive Week 2-window fantasy availability update. ([NFL Higgins report](https://www.nfl.com/news/texans-wr-jayden-higgins-torn-acl-out-2026-season))

### Tyler Biadasz, C, Chargers

Biadasz injured his left knee in the August 18 joint practice. The Chargers initially said imaging was complete and he was gathering additional opinions; the NFL's August 23 roundup later reported that he had been placed on season-ending injured reserve. This is offensive-line context rather than direct fantasy-player production. ([Chargers update](https://www.chargers.com/news/tyler-biadasz-injury-update-2026); [NFL Aug. 23 roundup](https://www.nfl.com/news/nfl-roundup-latest-league-news-from-sunday-aug-23))

### LeQuint Allen, RB, Jaguars

Allen was expected to miss the remainder of the preseason with what the official NFL recap called a soft-tissue injury. No more specific diagnosis or return date should be supplied from this source. ([NFL Friday recap](https://www.nfl.com/news/2026-nfl-preseason-week-2-what-we-learned-friday-games))

### Isaiah Bond, WR, Browns

Bond was evaluated for a concussion in the second quarter against Buffalo and subsequently cleared the protocol. Before leaving, he had one catch for eight yards and one rush for four yards. ([Browns Bond update](https://www.clevelandbrowns.com/news/isaiah-bond-being-evaluated-for-a-concussion))

### Johnny Newton, DT, Commanders

Washington confirmed before the Lions game that Newton had undergone successful pectoral surgery and would miss significant time; the team did not provide a return timetable. ([Commanders camp notes](https://www.commanders.com/news/commanders-training-camp-notes-starters-preseason-lions))

### Kam Dewberry, G, Falcons

Dewberry left the Colts game with trainers, was carted to the locker room and did not return. Atlanta's initial game report gave no diagnosis or recovery timetable. ([Falcons game breakdown](https://www.atlantafalcons.com/news/game-breakdown-what-happened-in-falcons-preseason-game-vs-indianapolis-colts))

### Bud Clark, S, Seahawks

Clark, Seattle's 2026 second-round pick, broke his ankle during the Titans game. Seahawks coach Mike Macdonald said Clark was expected to miss several months; an air cast was applied and Clark was carted off. ([NFL Aug. 23 roundup](https://www.nfl.com/news/nfl-roundup-latest-league-news-from-sunday-aug-23))

### Mason Richman, OL, Seahawks

Macdonald described Richman's injury from the Titans game as a major, long-term injury; the official roundup did not state a diagnosis or timetable. Do not add either without a later team update. ([NFL Aug. 23 roundup](https://www.nfl.com/news/nfl-roundup-latest-league-news-from-sunday-aug-23))

### Ashton Jeanty, RB, Raiders

Jeanty left the Raiders' August 23 practice unable to put pressure on his right leg after an apparent injury. At the time of the official roundup, no team-confirmed diagnosis or return timetable was provided, so article copy should not promote the apparent injury to a confirmed sprain without a newer first-party update. ([NFL Aug. 23 roundup](https://www.nfl.com/news/nfl-roundup-latest-league-news-from-sunday-aug-23))

### Christian McCaffrey, De'Zhaun Stribling and George Kittle, 49ers

McCaffrey returned to practice August 23 after missing multiple weeks with tightness; Stribling returned after a shoulder-related absence; and Kittle was activated from the physically unable to perform list following his Achilles recovery. These are availability updates around the Week 2 window, not Week 2 game injuries. ([NFL Aug. 23 roundup](https://www.nfl.com/news/nfl-roundup-latest-league-news-from-sunday-aug-23))

### Quinn Ewers and Trey Moore, Dolphins

Coach Jeff Hafley said Ewers had missed the preceding week with a groin injury and the Dolphins were hopeful he would return during the coming week. Hafley said Moore would miss time with the ankle injury he suffered against the Giants; the official report supplied no precise timetable. ([NFL Aug. 23 roundup](https://www.nfl.com/news/nfl-roundup-latest-league-news-from-sunday-aug-23))

### Danny Pinter, C, Ravens

Pinter was ruled out for the year with a torn patellar tendon before the Vikings game, leaving Baltimore to evaluate Jovaughn Gwyn and recovering veteran Ethan Pocic at center. This is a material line-context update for the offense, not an injury sustained during Week 2. ([NFL Saturday recap](https://www.nfl.com/news/2026-nfl-preseason-week-2-what-we-learned-saturday-games); [NFL Pinter report](https://www.nfl.com/news/ravens-center-danny-pinter-torn-patellar-tendon))

## Editorial limitations

- Preseason production comes against mixed personnel. Every fantasy conclusion should stay proportional to role, opponent unit and sample size.
- The NFL's recap pages provide selected Next Gen Stats context, not complete snap/route/pressure data for every player. Do not imply that the names above are an exhaustive leaguewide ranking.
- Official injury language was still developing on August 24. Recheck team updates immediately before publication, especially for Jeanty, Richman, Moore and Ewers.
- Do not call second-year players rookies: Cam Ward, Jacardia Wright, Tyrone Tracy Jr. and Joe Milton III are discussed for fantasy stock, not as members of the 2026 rookie class.
