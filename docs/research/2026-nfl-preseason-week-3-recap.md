## Fantasy draft-stock risers

### Zavion Thomas, WR, Bears: return-and-gadget arrow up

The third-round rookie caught a 97-yard touchdown from Tyson Bagent on the offense's first play from scrimmage, and finished with five catches for 140 yards in his first NFL game action. Next Gen Stats, cited in the NFL's recap, put the throw's air distance at 41.3 yards and Thomas' top speed at 21.99 mph. He also contributed in the return game.

Source conflict, do not paper over: the NFL's Saturday recap describes a 39-yard punt return; secondary accounts describe a 31-yard punt return plus a 22-yard kickoff return. Article copy therefore cites the receiving line and the 97-yard touchdown, which every source agrees on, and refers to return work without a contested yardage figure. ([NFL Saturday recap](https://www.nfl.com/news/2026-nfl-preseason-week-3-what-we-learned-from-saturday-s-games); [Bears highlight](https://www.chicagobears.com/video/can-t-miss-highlight-zavion-thomas-speeds-for-97-yard-td-on-first-play-from-scrimmage))

### Mike Washington Jr., RB, Raiders: contingency arrow up

Washington ran eight times for 49 yards against San Francisco and closed the preseason with 23 carries for 168 yards, a 7.3-yard average, with long runs of 53 and 33 yards in the first two games. The relevance is contingent: Ashton Jeanty is believed to have sprained his right ankle in practice, an injury NFL Network's Ian Rapoport and ESPN's Adam Schefter reported is not considered long-term, with no team-confirmed return timetable. Elevate Washington on a watchlist; do not draft him as if Jeanty is out. ([NFL Thursday recap](https://www.nfl.com/news/2026-nfl-preseason-week-3-what-we-learned-from-thursday-s-games); [NFL Jeanty report](https://www.nfl.com/news/raiders-rb-ashton-jeanty-apparent-right-leg-injury))

### Cody Schrader, RB, Broncos: NOT a riser, he was cut

Schrader produced 19 touches for 132 yards, six catches for 90 yards and 13 carries for 42 yards, and caught a fourth-down touchdown from Sam Ehlinger.

**Correction applied 2026-09-01 after adversarial QA.** An earlier draft listed Schrader as a fantasy stock riser with a passing-down role. Denver waived him on August 30 while setting its initial 53 (Dobbins, Harvey, Coleman and Badie are the active-roster backs) and re-signed him to the practice squad on August 31. The article now uses him as the lead example in the "Audition Tape" section, which is the accurate framing: a 132-yard finale did not produce a roster spot. This is the single best illustration of why finale production must be checked against the cutdown before it reaches a draft board. ([NFL Friday recap](https://www.nfl.com/news/2026-nfl-preseason-week-3-what-we-learned-friday-games); [Broncos video](https://www.denverbroncos.com/video/sam-ehlinger-scrambles-tosses-fourth-down-td-pass-to-cody-schrader); [Mile High Report on the waiver](https://www.milehighreport.com/denver-broncos-roster/183975/denver-broncos-have-waived-running-back-cody-schrader); [practice-squad re-signing](https://heavy.com/sports/nfl/denver-broncos/cody-schrader-signing/))

Same failure mode, same fix: rookie TE **Justin Joly**, who caught Ehlinger's 88-yard touchdown, was waived by Denver on August 30 and claimed by Miami. The article states that outcome inline rather than leaving the name as an unqualified standout. ([Mile High Report](https://www.milehighreport.com/denver-broncos-news/183880/broncos-waive-rookie-tight-end-justin-joly))

### Skyler Bell, WR, Bills: roster-battle arrow up

The fourth-round rookie made his NFL debut after a groin injury cost him the first two preseason weeks, and led Buffalo with seven catches on 10 targets for 49 yards, adding a 28-yard touchdown on his only carry, a jet sweep. Buffalo won 28-27 on a late two-point conversion. Volume with a low yards-per-catch is a role signal, not a production forecast. Cutdown check: Bell made Buffalo's initial 53. ([NFL Thursday recap](https://www.nfl.com/news/2026-nfl-preseason-week-3-what-we-learned-from-thursday-s-games); [Bills takeaways](https://www.buffalobills.com/news/top-3-things-we-learned-bills-vs-steelers-preseason-week-3-2026))

### J. Michael Sturdivant and Will Sheppard, WRs, Packers: deep-roster arrows up

In a 42-38 finale, Sturdivant caught six passes for 112 yards including a 29-yard touchdown, finishing as Green Bay's preseason receiving leader at 142 yards on nine catches. Sheppard caught three passes for 26 yards and returned a punt 81 yards for a score.

Cutdown check: Sturdivant, undrafted, made Green Bay's initial 53; Sheppard was released and re-signed to the practice squad. The article states both outcomes.

The punt-return yardage was initially treated as a conflict (an aggregator headline said 80), but packers.com and the NFL recap independently say 81, so 81 is used. ([NFL Friday recap](https://www.nfl.com/news/2026-nfl-preseason-week-3-what-we-learned-friday-games); [Packers takeaways](https://www.packers.com/news/game-recap-5-takeaways-from-packers-preseason-win-over-cardinals-pre-week-3-2026))

### Ty Simpson, QB, Rams: developmental arrow up

The No. 13 overall pick completed 18 of 23 for 119 yards with a touchdown and no interceptions, and closed the preseason 47 of 60 for 382 yards with three touchdowns and no interceptions. Sean McVay had not settled the QB2 job between Simpson and Stetson Bennett. This is a dynasty and superflex signal only. ([NFL Thursday recap](https://www.nfl.com/news/2026-nfl-preseason-week-3-what-we-learned-from-thursday-s-games); [Rams recap](https://www.therams.com/news/quarterback-ty-simpson-decisive-composed-and-accurate-in-rams-preseason-finale-win-against-chargers))

### Dillon Gabriel, QB, Browns: trade-value arrow up, fantasy arrow flat

Gabriel went 12 of 15 for 186 yards with three first-half touchdowns and a 157.9 passer rating. Todd Monken had already named Deshaun Watson the 2026 starter on August 24, so the performance reads as an audition for another club rather than a path to Cleveland snaps. Gabriel was subsequently placed on injured reserve with a designation to return because of a back injury, which removes the near-term fantasy case entirely. Do not present him as a startable quarterback. ([NFL Thursday recap](https://www.nfl.com/news/2026-nfl-preseason-week-3-what-we-learned-from-thursday-s-games))

## Fantasy draft-stock fallers

### Fernando Mendoza, QB, Raiders: second straight rough outing

The No. 1 overall pick went 7 of 14 for 57 yards with an interception, was sacked twice and posted a 31.0 passer rating against San Francisco, after a Week 2 line that included an 80-yard pick-six. Context still applies: he is a rookie behind a reshuffled line, and Kirk Cousins went 8 of 11 for 51 yards ahead of him in the same game. The conclusion is developmental patience, not a redraft investment. ([NFL Thursday recap](https://www.nfl.com/news/2026-nfl-preseason-week-3-what-we-learned-from-thursday-s-games))

### Makai Lemon, WR, Eagles: rough debut, unchanged projected role

Philadelphia's first-round rookie debuted after a hamstring injury cost him most of camp, caught three of five targets for three yards, and had a drop that led directly to an interception, in a 30-13 loss to Cincinnati.

Two precision points from adversarial QA: (1) the punt was not a muff. The Inquirer describes him letting a fluttering punt bounce, the ball caroming between his legs, and Lemon recovering it himself. No turnover. Article copy says "a botched punt-fielding decision he had to recover himself." (2) Do not state his regular-season role as settled. The Inquirer says it is "unclear how extensively the Eagles will use Lemon during the regular season," and other reporting had Dontayvion Wicks leading for the No. 2 job while Lemon was out. Article copy names Smith and Wicks as the established pieces and says Philadelphia has not stated his usage. ([NFL Friday recap](https://www.nfl.com/news/2026-nfl-preseason-week-3-what-we-learned-friday-games); [Philadelphia Inquirer](https://www.inquirer.com/eagles/first-round-wide-receiver-makai-lemon-preseason-debut-20260829.html))

### Will Levis, QB, Titans: no separation

With Cam Ward and Mitchell Trubisky held out, Levis went 14 of 22 for 143 yards in a half, converted 1 of 6 third downs and fumbled a receiver handoff. Tennessee lost 24-15. ([NFL Saturday recap](https://www.nfl.com/news/2026-nfl-preseason-week-3-what-we-learned-from-saturday-s-games))

### Tua Tagovailoa, QB, Falcons: efficient line, no downfield evidence

Tagovailoa returned to Miami and went 7 of 8 for 95 yards, but did not complete a pass longer than 10 air yards and lost a fumble on a snap exchange. Atlanta had NOT named a Week 1 starter: this is an open competition between Tagovailoa and Michael Penix Jr., and head coach Kevin Stefanski held Penix out of the finale because Penix had completed only three full-team practices. Do not write "behind Penix" or otherwise imply Penix is the incumbent; the club's own breakdown says the starter question "has not yet taken the form of an answer." Kyle Pitts caught all three of his targets for 59 yards, including a 37-yard tight end screen. ([NFL Friday recap](https://www.nfl.com/news/2026-nfl-preseason-week-3-what-we-learned-friday-games); [Falcons breakdown](https://www.atlantafalcons.com/news/game-breakdown-what-happened-falcons-dolphins-preseason-final-score-takeaways))

### Jalon Daniels, QB, Buccaneers: shut out

Daniels completed 8 of 15 for 57 yards across seven drives in a 19-0 loss, with Jake Browning adding 2 of 4 for 2 yards. Tampa Bay went 2 of 12 on third down and gave up five sacks.

Outcome, which the article must state rather than leave hanging: Tampa Bay named Daniels its QB2 behind Baker Mayfield anyway, on his full-August body of work (27 of 44, 239 yards, one touchdown, no interceptions across the preseason), and released Browning. ([NFL report](https://www.nfl.com/news/undrafted-rookie-jalon-daniels-earns-bucs-qb2-gig-behind-baker-mayfield)) ([NFL Friday recap](https://www.nfl.com/news/2026-nfl-preseason-week-3-what-we-learned-friday-games); [Jaguars key plays](https://www.jaguars.com/news/five-key-plays-in-jaguars-19-0-win-over-buccaneers-in-2026-preseason-finale))

## Adversarial QA pass, 2026-09-01

Three adversarial reviewers were run against the drafted article before publication: a fact-checker working from primary sources, a house-style and editorial reviewer, and an integration reviewer that mutation-tested the wiring. Material corrections applied, recorded here so the next recap does not repeat them:

- **Check the cutdown, not just the box score.** Cody Schrader and Justin Joly were both waived on August 30 after standout Friday performances. An article published September 1 that calls a waived player a fantasy riser is wrong on its own terms. Every named standout in this article was checked against the initial 53.
- **Do not paraphrase a coach into a quote.** A drafted line attributed a "most entertaining preseason game" characterization to Matt LaFleur from an aggregator summary. Cut.
- **Every stat line needs a link, not just a source.** The "Audition Tape" bullets were sourced but unlinked. Fixed.
- **Do not promise more sourcing rigor than you deliver.** The Reporting Notes claimed official NFL or club sourcing throughout while two injury entries were NBC Sports. The notes now name the exception.
- **Avoid leaguewide superlatives** ("the loudest first impression any rookie made", "the cleanest stash on the board"). The recap pages are selective, so they cannot support a ranking.
- **Do not assert market movement.** "Discount" was rewritten to "underpriced against his draft capital", which is an editorial judgment rather than an ADP claim.
- **readMinutes must track word count.** Weeks 1 and 2 imply roughly 124 words per minute; this article is about 2,450 prose words, so 20 is right and the drafted 13 was not.

## Editorial limitations

- Week 3 is the starters-rested week. Nearly every performance above came against mixed or reserve personnel, and several came against players who were cut two days later. Weight accordingly.
- The NFL's recap pages provide selected Next Gen Stats context, not complete snap, route or pressure data for every player. The names above are not a leaguewide ranking.
- One numeric conflict remains and is handled by omission rather than by picking a side: Zavion Thomas' return yardage (the NFL recap says a 39-yard punt return, secondary accounts say a 31-yard punt return plus a 22-yard kickoff return), so only his receiving line appears in the article. Sheppard's punt return resolved to 81 once packers.com was read directly. Bryan Thomas Jr.'s pressure-versus-hit count is handled by attribution.
- Roster and injury language was still moving on September 1. Recheck team updates immediately before publication, especially for Jeanty, Ossai, Kamara and Jacobs.
- Do not call non-rookies rookies. Tua Tagovailoa, Will Levis, Tyson Bagent, Sam Ehlinger, Cody Schrader, Mike Washington Jr., Tyrone Tracy Jr., Dillon Gabriel and Sauce Gardner are discussed for fantasy or roster reasons, not as members of the 2026 rookie class.
- Josh Jacobs' exempt-list status involves pending legal matters. State the roster status, do not characterize the underlying allegations.
