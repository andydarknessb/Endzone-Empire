# 2026 rookie class: camp through preseason Week 1

Research file for the `rookie-draft-round-guide` strategy article (#74). Built 2026-08-19 by a six-agent fan-out: one class-table barrier plus five research axes (tier1, tier2, market, injuries, camp).

**Current as of 2026-08-19.** Preseason Week 1 was played Aug 13-15. Preseason Week 2 (Aug 20-23) had NOT been played when this was gathered, so every usage figure here rests on a one-game sample.

Consolidated by script, not by a model: every claim below is reproduced verbatim from the agent that retrieved it, with its source link and its own VERIFIED or UNCERTAIN label. Nothing was summarised, merged or promoted. Where two axes disagree, both claims appear next to each other under the player.

## How to use this file

1. No player claim goes in the article without a line in this file.
2. UNCERTAIN never becomes a flat statement in the article. Either verify it further or attribute the uncertainty in the prose.
3. Check every name against the class table below before calling anyone a rookie. The traps table exists because a prior scout got this wrong repeatedly.
