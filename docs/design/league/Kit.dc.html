League kit

Pieces added to the Home kit for every /league/:id/* page. Square corners, 6px double frames, no shadows, stepped motion.

Position tags and grade chips are labels first: the letters carry the meaning and colour is a second cue. Each has a contrast pair on Field, Panel and Board. Never use them as decoration.

Table and standings

Sticky first column inside an overflow-x box, tabular figures, 44px rows with 2px Rule dividers, no zebra. The viewer row is Play-blue with an arrow and is never the cursor. The cut line is a dashed Frame rule with a label.

Versus panel and scoreboard

Before kickoff: framed, projected figures, Frame-colour odds bar. Live: the Board-black tile scaled up, leader white and trailer dim, the margin directly under the viewer score, live odds bar, the score block is one link. The score flash is a specimen: the wash on the live tile below is the flash held at rest under reduced motion, and no dashboard board carries it.

Team, recap, around the league, commissioner

Every panel is the one frame with a 2px Rule divider under its head. The commissioner strip is never filled; its only red is a lock under one hour.

Playbook tiles

Tiles are Play-blue with a 96 by 48 pictogram and never carry a filled foot. On subpages the one cursor sits on a row with the same 4px frame as the lead play card; the Lineup section below shows it once.

Form controls

Native inputs, restyled. 44px targets, 2px borders, Dim border at rest and Ink on hover and focus, the white focus ring with the dark halo. Error is a Danger border plus a message with an icon, never colour alone.

Dialog, menu, tabs, breadcrumb

Dialog is a 6px double Frame on a Field scrim. Menu is a framed list: hover is Play-blue, the selected item has an arrow. Tabs only appear where a page already has tabs. The breadcrumb restyles the existing back link and adds no navigation.

Chat and activity

The chat button is 56px, square, with a 28px count badge; the drawer docks 380px on desktop and goes full width on a phone. Activity rows are Archivo sentences with a square kind tag.

Trophies and countdown

Trophies are pixel pictograms on a 2px grid in Frame colour: a cup for season titles, a star for weekly awards. The countdown is Pixel 32 in square blocks.

Matchup status legend and skeleton blocks

In progress is a filled square, Final an outline square, Yet to play an outline with a centre dot: three shapes, always beside the words. Skeleton blocks are square Skel fills that do not animate; the container sets aria-busy.

Draft room

Pieces of the Draft room board. The pick banner carries the status, the clock and nothing else: no round meter. Rail controls are 44px and the dense table keeps 36px buttons. Move arrows are 16px on the 2px grid and sit in their own pair, apart from the 12px remove x. Every chat message has a quiet Hide.

Lineup

Each ledger row is one button (aria-pressed) with the player name and Drop player as separate buttons on top. The cursor row, the one live cursor on this sheet, is the player who needs a move. During a move, eligible rows turn Play-blue and ineligible rows keep full opacity: Dim text, a dashed rule and "not eligible" for screen readers.
