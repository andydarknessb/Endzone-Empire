What the numbers mean
Spread
The favorite and the points it is expected to win by.
O/U
The expected combined score of both teams.
FAV
Marks the favorite, the team the spread expects to win.
Win probability
ESPN's live figure for each team's chance to win; it shows on live games.
Confidence
Each pick carries a rank from 1 up to the week's game count, each used once. A correct pick earns its rank.

A rank held by a game that has kicked off cannot be swapped: its menu row reads 'Confidence 9 · locked, PHI @ MIN', is aria-disabled and cannot be chosen, and its rank-pool cell carries a small lock (drawn on the States sheet; nothing has kicked off on this board).
