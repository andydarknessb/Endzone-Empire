Save on tap (Critique 10, a product change for spec #2334): choosing a team saves that pick at once, so the sheet has no batch-save button, no dirty state, no dashed edge, no leave-confirmation dialog and no bottom bar. Every open card carries a status line under its teams, empty at rest, and that line alone is a polite live region (role=status): it holds the words only, and the ghost Undo and Retry buttons sit beside it, outside the region, so a tap makes one announcement (the Picks h2 status and the rank strip's words are plain text). Saving: 'Saving…' in Dim, and the chosen team button is outlined in Play-blue (a 4px edge, no fill) with an empty square and 'Pick' instead of the check: saving is not saved. Saved: the fill and the pixel check arrive with 'Pick saved' and a ghost 'Undo' that restores the previous pick (or no pick). Failed: the alert pictogram and 'Couldn't save this pick.' in Danger, a ghost 'Retry' (aria-label 'Retry saving pick, DAL at DEN') and the attempted team still outlined, so a refused pick never looks saved. A failed or kicked-off card wears a 2px Danger edge and the alert pictogram; the 4px frame belongs to the cursor alone. Locked games cannot change.Team buttons are named '{ABBR}, {Team} over {Opponent}, {day time}{, locks in n min}', so the name starts with the visible abbreviation (for example 'KC, Kansas City Chiefs over Las Vegas Raiders, Sun 3:25 PM, locks in 45 min'), and add the state where it matters: 'saving', 'saved' or 'not saved, retry' (the one place those words survive, in an accessible name). Kicked off before save: the card is drawn Locked (lock icon, Locked chip, kickoff past) with a 2px Danger edge and the alert line 'This pick wasn't saved: DAL @ DEN had already kicked off. Your other picks are saved.'; no team is filled, since nothing was saved.Confidence saves on tap too. Choosing a rank saves it. Choosing a used rank swaps the two games' ranks, both saved, and each card says 'Swapped with {the other game}' with 'Both ranks saved' and a ghost 'Undo swap'; the specimen's tally uses Sunday Pick'em's real size, 10 managers. A rank held by a game that has kicked off cannot be swapped (Critique 11): its option reads 'Confidence 9 · locked, PHI @ MIN' (disabled in the native select, aria-disabled in the listbox mirror, dimmed with a lock, never highlighted) and its rank-pool cell carries a small lock above the number; if a stale menu still sends it, the server answers 409 and the card says 'Rank 9 is locked to PHI @ MIN.' with 'Choose another rank.'
