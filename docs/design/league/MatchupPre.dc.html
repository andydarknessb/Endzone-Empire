What the numbers mean
Proj
A lineup's points before its games are played, from Endzone Forecast, our projection engine.
Expected final
Points so far plus the projected points still to come: a starter's projection times the share of his game left, or all of it before his game starts.
Players remaining
Starters whose NFL game isn't final. Out starters and empty slots don't count. Before kickoff it reads "starters set to play".
Yet to play
The players remaining whose NFL game hasn't started.
Win probability
Your chance to win the matchup from both lineups' projected finals (Endzone Forecast). It sharpens as games finish.
Projected margin
How far one lineup is projected to finish ahead of the other: the gap between the two Proj totals before kickoff, between the two Expected finals once games are live.
