Roster-capacity and position-cap refusals arrive when a trade executes (accept or force approve), so they surface on the page, not in this dialog (TradesStates).Focus (spec): opening the dialog puts focus on the 'Trade with' select (on a Counter, where the select is disabled, on the first You send checkbox; on a deep link, on the first You send checkbox, since the team is preselected). Closing by Cancel, Escape or a successful send returns focus to the opener: the Propose Trade button, or the card's Counter button, which has left Pending after a send, so focus falls to the next card's heading (the Trades notes) and the toast announces.
