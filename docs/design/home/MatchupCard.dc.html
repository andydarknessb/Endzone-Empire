Four specimens of one league card. Before kickoff is the full panel with Projected scores and the win probability bar. Live on Home is the scoreboard tile from the game-day board: the score block links to the Matchup page, and the league's other links sit under it. The full live card (score, projected final, players remaining) lives on the Matchup page.Final panels show the winner's score in ink and the loser's in dim, the result in words in the pixel face, and a visually hidden sentence with the full result. Both chips are ink: green is only for the one filled action and live state, and a loss is not an error, so never red. A win also gets the trophy sprite beside the chip, in ink; a loss gets the chip alone. The Thursday Night Regulars win adds its pick'em line ("Picks this week 9-5") in dim, because that pick'em is straight-up.Final panels keep every link the league has, in the canonical order: Thursday Night Regulars shows Matchup, Lineup, Picks, Waivers; Office League shows Matchup, Lineup, Waivers. The before-kickoff panel is the Thursday Night Regulars panel from Home, its odds bar is frame-coloured because the game has not started, and its header carries "times in CDT".
